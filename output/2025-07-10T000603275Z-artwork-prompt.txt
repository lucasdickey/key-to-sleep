In this artwork, Alex, the eleven-year-old nonbinary child, stands with their otter companion, Nori, as the central focal points. The setting is a serene coastal shoreline during twilight, capturing the essence of a peaceful evening by the sea. 

Alex is depicted wearing an APES ON KEYS hoodie, the warm fabric slightly billowing with the gentle ocean breeze. Their expression is one of calm contentment, eyes gently focused on the distant horizon. Barefoot, Alex stands on the cool, damp sand, feeling the earth's texture beneath their feet. The hoodie is a soft olive green, blending harmoniously with the muted, vintage-inspired color palette of the scene.

Nori, the otter, is shown playfully at Alex's side. Her sleek fur catches the sunset's last golden rays, glistening like polished bronze. She is in motion, perhaps nudging a small shell with her nose, her whiskers twitching in delight. Nori's presence adds a sense of playful curiosity to the serene setting.

The background features the ocean, its waves gently lapping against the shore, reflecting hues of sunset orange, pink, and deepening blues. The sky is a tranquil gradient, transitioning from a warm golden glow near the horizon to a deeper indigo dotted with the first twinkling stars. To the right, the crescent moon begins to rise, casting a soft silver light over the scene.

The composition is balanced with ample negative space, allowing the viewer to breathe in the tranquility of the scene. The flat design and bold line work evoke a retro, mid-century children's book aesthetic, complemented by a subtle grain that adds a vintage texture to the illustration. The peaceful, dreamlike quality of the artwork invites viewers to immerse themselves in the quiet adventure and gentle wonder of Alex and Nori's evening journey along the shoreline.

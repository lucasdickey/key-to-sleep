The artwork captures a serene coastal scene at twilight, invoking a sense of peace and connection with nature. Central to the composition is Sky, an eleven-year-old nonbinary child, sitting comfortably on a smooth rock near the shoreline. Sky wears an "APES ON KEYS" hoodie, which adds a touch of individuality and modernity to the vintage-inspired illustration. 

Beside Sky is Ripple, a playful otter whose sleek body glistens with the last light of the day. Ripple's eyes are bright, reflecting the calm sea, and he appears mid-motion, either playfully rolling in the sand or poised to dart back into the water. The bond between them is evident in their shared gaze, conveying a silent understanding and companionship.

The setting envelops the characters in a tranquil ambience with the sun having dipped low, casting a warm glow of pinks, oranges, and deepening blues across the sky. The horizon is subtly blurred, giving the sea a dreamlike quality as it stretches infinitely into the distance. The gentle waves lap against the shore, creating delicate patterns in the sand, which is dotted with small, iridescent shells and smooth stones.

In the background, distant silhouettes of gulls glide effortlessly, adding to the calming atmosphere. The air seems to carry the scent of salt and seaweed, captured through soft, muted tones and a light texture overlay that mimics the grain of vintage posters. The overall color palette is warm and subdued, with hints of twilight purples starting to emerge, enhancing the serene, sleepy mood.

The illustration invites the viewer into a peaceful moment, emphasizing the connection between Sky, Ripple, and the natural world around them. It evokes a sense of calm and wonder, perfect for guiding one into restful slumber.
